## Slutprompt panorama

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one continuous extra-wide high-resolution 3:1 panorama for the background of a Swedish 2D flight game, NORRUT. Requested canvas 3840 by 1280 pixels, landscape panorama. Nordic semi-realistic painterly indie game art with crisp fine detail, warm late afternoon sunlight. One coherent panoramic view across Dalälven near Avesta, Sweden, looking north across a long river valley. Sky fills the upper 48 percent, layered distant low wooded blue hills from left to right, forests of pine, spruce and birch, long tranquil blue river running continuously across the lower third, natural banks with grasses and tiny wildflowers. Some tiny traditional red wooden houses can appear along the opposite bank but no large landmark buildings, no industrial chimney, no roads in foreground; game will add those as separate world objects. Composition is unbroken, no panels, no frames, no cut edges, no collaged scenes, no dramatic perspective vanishing point. Left and right edges should match in horizon height, riverbank elevation, lighting and tree density for seamless repeating at very long distances. Do not place large foreground trees, branches or leaves. Leave lower 8 percent softly forested and grassy to blend with the game's ground. No text, no UI, no watermark. Detailed foliage and natural colors, clear water reflections, fine atmospheric depth, broad open sky suitable for an independently animated sun, gentle clouds and changing daylight. The illustration must remain sharp when a moving camera shows only a portion of the panorama.

## Slutprompt björkförgrund

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent foreground decorative birch foliage asset for Swedish 2D flight game NORRUT. One irregular natural cluster of delicate birch branches entering from the UPPER LEFT corner, extending towards center and downward along LEFT edge only. Green and golden leaves in warm sunlight, fine elegant twigs, some gaps between leaves. Semi-realistic Nordic painterly indie game style, very detailed crisp foliage, coherent 2D illustrated realism. Genuine alpha transparency everywhere except leaves, twigs and a very narrow birch trunk at far left. Outer leaf silhouette must be entirely organic with gently tapering sparse tips; no hard rectangular edge, no bottom straight edge, no landscape background, no ground, no grass, no sky, no frame. The leaf cluster should occupy the upper-left half with the lower-right two-thirds mostly transparent, so it can gently sway over a parallax landscape without blocking gameplay. Wide landscape canvas with transparent right and bottom margins. No text, no watermark.
