# Miljöbilder för NORRUT! 0.5

Skapade med det inbyggda bildgenereringsverktyget, därefter konverterade till WebP utan ändring av motiv.

Projektfil: `public/art/route-atlas.webp` (1254 × 1254). Åtta bildrutor i två kolumner och fyra rader: Hedemora, Säter, Borlänge, Falun, odlingslandskap, skog, Verket och Dalahästen. Startplatsen använder befintlig `landscape.webp`. Stadsmiljöerna är konstnärliga tolkningar, inte kartografiska eller fotografiska rekonstruktioner. Inga geografiska exakthetsanspråk görs. Spelrutten går från Avesta mot Hedemora, Säter, Borlänge och Falun; avstånd är spelvärldens ungefärliga ruttavstånd.

## Bildprompt

Create ONE production raster game background ATLAS for the Swedish side-view flying game NORRUT!, semi-realistic Nordic indie painted illustration, detailed warm summer Scandinavian light, blue sky with white clouds, believable red timber buildings, lush mixed forest. This is NOT a game screenshot. No UI, text, numbers, aircraft, people, logos, borders, margins or labels. Exact layout: TWO columns by FOUR rows, eight equal rectangular panoramic cells in a square 2048x2048 image. Each cell is a separate seamless-to-itself scenic background with its horizon at 60 percent down and clear blue sky occupying top half, camera across landscape from side, ground base at 85 percent. Keep all cells precisely aligned in their grid with hard straight boundaries, no blending between cells. Row1 left: Hedemora historic small town in Dalarna with wooden merchants houses and Swedish church clock tower, pastoral hill. Row1 right: Säter old wooden town, pale church steeple, gardens and birches, distinctly different skyline. Row2 left: Borlänge Domnarvet steelworks along the broad Dalälven river with bridges, industrial chimneys, modern low city skyline. Row2 right: Falun copper mine, rust red open pit terraces and historic wooden mining buildings, church skyline in distance. Row3 left: quiet rolling Dalarna farmland, yellow meadow, red barn, lake, no town skyline. Row3 right: continuous pine and birch forest, rocky hills and small lake, no architecture. Row4 left: Avesta old ironworks Verket brick halls and tall chimney beside Dalälven and red old village houses, no giant horse. Row4 right: Avesta giant decorated red Dala horse monument standing on low green hill beside birches, open fields. All eight cells should be distinct locally inspired landscapes, painterly with detailed textures but clear silhouettes, gentle atmosphere, no photograph collage. Draw natural countryside ground without an asphalt parking lot. Make an attractive high-quality raster asset suitable behind animated 2D sprites.
